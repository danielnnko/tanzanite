Raw barometric altitude has sensor bias. Use "Zero Altitude" while the device sits at a known height above the ground station. This is a sensor calibration only — it does not change your surveyed GS position above.

Attitude Filter Tuning

Slow 3D attitude response is usually a gyro-unit mismatch. Verify first: rotate the satellite by hand — the ω readouts on the Attitude View should reach tens to hundreds of °/s. If they stay below 1, raise the gyro scale until they match the rotation speed.

Accelerometer trust per packet. Lower = snappier: 0.98 ≈ 50 packets to converge, 0.95 ≈ 20, 0.90 ≈ 10.

Multiplier applied to gyro_x/y/z. Examples: firmware in rad/s → 57.2958; raw 16-bit IMU at ±1000 dps → 0.0305. If the firmware already sends °/s, keep at 1.
